LUYEN DOC TIENG VIET LOP 2 - DUA LEN RAILWAY

Muc tieu: co mot duong link cong khai de gui cho hoc sinh, khong can Manus.

1. Tao tai khoan Railway va dang nhap bang Google/GitHub.
2. Tao mot repository GitHub moi, vi du: luyendoc2.
3. Giai nen file ZIP nay tren may. Tai len GitHub TOAN BO NOI DUNG BEN TRONG thu muc luyendoc2_work (khong tai nguyen ca thu muc boc ngoai).
4. Tren Railway chon New Project -> Deploy from GitHub Repo -> chon repository luyendoc2.
5. Railway se doc Dockerfile va build ung dung.
6. Vao service -> Variables, tao:
   NODE_ENV=production
   JWT_SECRET=<mot chuoi bi mat dai>
   TEACHER_PIN=<ma PIN rieng cua co>
   DATA_DIR=/app/data
   OPENAI_API_KEY=<bo trong neu chua dung AI>
   FPT_TTS_API_KEY=<bo trong neu chua dung FPT TTS>
7. Vao service -> Volumes -> tao volume 0.5 GB (Free/Trial) hoac toi da 5 GB tren Hobby, mount path: /app/data.
8. Deploy lai.
9. Vao Settings/Networking -> Generate Domain. Railway se cap mot link https://....up.railway.app
10. Mo link do tren may tinh va dien /api/health. Neu thay {"ok":true} la server da chay.
11. Gui link cho hoc sinh.

LUU Y QUAN TRONG
- Volume /app/data la noi giu db.json va audio; neu khong gan volume, du lieu local co the mat khi container bi thay moi.
- Khong dua OPENAI_API_KEY vao ma nguon GitHub. Chi dat trong Railway Variables.
- Khong can Manus de website hoat dong.
- SpeechRecognition tieng Viet van chay tren trinh duyet hoc sinh; mot so trinh duyet co the yeu cau HTTPS, vi vay dung link https Railway.

CHI PHI
Railway hien co goi Free/Trial cho viec bat dau va volume 0.5 GB; sau giai doan thu, trang pricing hien neu dung goi Free thi co muc $1/thang, con Hobby co muc toi thieu $5/thang. Kiem tra gia hien hanh tren Railway truoc khi nang cap.
