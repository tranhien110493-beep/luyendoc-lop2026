Luyen Doc Tieng Viet Lop 2 - Ban doc lap Manus

1) Ban nay KHONG phu thuoc Manus.
   - Khong Manus OAuth
   - Khong Manus Forge
   - Khong Manus Storage
   - Khong Manus hosting/runtime

2) Chay tren may Windows
   - Cai Node.js 20+.
   - Mo file START-WINDOWS.bat.
   - Lan dau chuong trinh se cai thu vien.
   - Mo http://localhost:3000

3) AI
   - Neu khong dien OPENAI_API_KEY: website van chay, dung cau hoi/nhan xet du phong.
   - Neu muon AI tao cau hoi va nhan xet: mo file .env va dien OPENAI_API_KEY.

4) FPT.AI
   - Neu muon tao audio bai doc/nhan xet bang FPT.AI: dien FPT_TTS_API_KEY.
   - Micro doc bai cua hoc sinh dung SpeechRecognition cua trinh duyet, khong qua Manus.

5) Du lieu
   - data/db.json: bai giao + ket qua hoc sinh.
   - data/uploads/: audio co giao tai len.

6) Khi dua len hosting
   - Hosting phai chay duoc Node.js/Express hoac Docker.
   - Dat NODE_ENV=production, PORT=3000.
   - Dat JWT_SECRET va TEACHER_PIN.
   - Neu dung AI/TTS thi dat OPENAI_API_KEY/FPT_TTS_API_KEY.
   - Khong can tai khoan Manus.
